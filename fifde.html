What is an FDE?

A Forward Deployed Engineer is becoming a hot job. You build and ship enterprise AI inside a client's world, find the real problem before writing code, and stay until the client's team uses it without you.

Why FDE, why now

AI demos are everywhere. Working AI isn't.

24weeks across two phases
1+year of programming experience is the only entry bar
12principles of a great FDE

FDE = AI engineer + a slice of AI PM. Phase 1 builds the tech. Phase 2 lands it on the client floor.

Phase 1: TechPython, RAG, agents, ERP data, DevOps, LLMOps, system design.
Phase 2: ProductDiscovery, BRD, TDD, stakeholders, UAT, change management.
Bonus: SAPOptional depth for SAP and field-service clients.
